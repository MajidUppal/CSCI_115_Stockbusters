It's important to note that throughout these sections on the time value of money and compounded or discounted values of mixed streams and their analysis, we are placing the valuation at the end or beginning of a period for simplicity in the examples. However, because this is a technique of forecasting, which is inherently uncertain, we will continue with analysis by period.
