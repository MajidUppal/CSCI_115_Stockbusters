Thus, if interest rates rise because of changing market conditions, the price of bonds will fall. The sensitivity of bond prices to changes in the interest rate is known as interest rate risk. Duration is an important measure of interest rate risk that incorporates the maturity and coupon rate of a bond as well as the level of current market interest rates.
