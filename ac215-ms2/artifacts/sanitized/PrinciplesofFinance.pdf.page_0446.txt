Measures of Slope and Intercept from Regression Analysis An important application of regression analysis is to determine the systematic risk for a particular stock, which is referred to as beta. A stock's beta is a measure of the volatility of the stock compared to a benchmark such as the S&P 500 index. If a stock has more volatility compared to the benchmark, then the stock will have a beta greater than 1.0.
