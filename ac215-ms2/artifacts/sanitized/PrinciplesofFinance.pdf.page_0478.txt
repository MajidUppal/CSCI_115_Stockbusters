Finance theory suggests that investors who purchase DAL will expect a higher rate of return to compensate them for this risk. There are several reasons why beta may vary slightly from source to source. One is the time frame used in the beta calculation. Data from three years were used to calculate the beta in Figure 15.3. Time frames ranging from three to five years are commonly used when calculating beta. Another reason different sources might report different betas is the frequency with which the data is collected. Monthly returns are used in Figure 15.3; some analysts will use weekly data.
